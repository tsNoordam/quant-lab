The only survivor so far. The trend-free spread version fails (phase 3b), because the whole curve falls before mid-month auctions; what remains is a directional duration short. Caveats: the split by auction cycle was found after looking (post-hoc), overlapping positions inflate t-stats somewhat, and rallies in auction weeks cost money (2011, 2014, 2023). Risk sizing gives about 0.3 contract per auction on $100k: micro futures or a small size. Next: demo forward test.
